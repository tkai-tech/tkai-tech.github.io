Solar mit Gewinn

From roof area to a sound decision: Solar mit Gewinn forecasts yields from real site data and calculates the payback period with and without storage, honestly, instead of relying on gut feeling and sales promises. The calculator is in German and built for the German market.

Open the calculator: solarmitgewinn.de →

What the tool does

  • System and storage: System size in kWp and an optional battery feed directly into the calculation.
  • Yield forecast: Based on real irradiation and site data instead of flat estimates.
  • Economics: Payback period, self-consumption rate and feed-in tariff calculated transparently, including scenarios.
  • Quote comparison: Put existing quotes into perspective and compare them objectively.
