About tkai

tkai is the company of Thomas Koormann from Lingen (Ems), Germany. Development, operation and data protection are in one hand. AI is a tool here, not a selling point.

  • Talking instead of typing. We believe that in the future we will mostly interact with AI through speech. Plauderherz already works this way: no menus, no keyboard, just a conversation.
  • Honest numbers. The calculators show their assumptions and data sources. If something does not pay off, they say so just as clearly.
  • Sparing with data. No advertising cookies, no tracking. The products run on servers in Germany.
  • Open about the technology. Which services are used for what is listed on a separate page: Tools and services.
