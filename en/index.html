Who is behind it

I am Thomas Koormann from Lingen (Ems), Germany, and I develop the tools at tkai.tech. From the first idea to day-to-day operation, I am your point of contact.

  • Talking instead of typing. I believe that in the future we will mostly interact with AI through speech. Plauderherz already works this way: no menus, no keyboard, just a conversation.
  • Honest numbers. The calculators show their assumptions and data sources. If something does not pay off, they say so just as clearly.
  • Sparing with data. No advertising cookies, no tracking. The products run on servers in Germany.
  • Open about the technology. Which services are used for what is listed on a separate page: Tools and services.
