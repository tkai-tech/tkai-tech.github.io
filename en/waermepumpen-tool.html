Wärmepumpe mit Gewinn

Digital heat pump planning without waiting weeks for quotes: a few building details produce a sound system design including an economic analysis. Clarity before the installer arrives. The calculator is in German and built for the German market.

Open the calculator: waermepumpemitgewinn.de →

What the tool does

  • Heat load and sizing: Calculates the right heat pump size from a few details about the building, insulation and heating habits.
  • Economics: An honest cost comparison with the existing heating system, including subsidies.
  • Clear report: A planning document that homeowners understand and can hand straight to the installer.
  • Quote check: Assess existing quotes instead of trusting them blindly.
