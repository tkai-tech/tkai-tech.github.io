EngiCalc

A calculation platform for mechanical and plant engineering: 24 calculators in six disciplines, each with a traceable calculation path. The calculation core is open source. The platform interface is in German.

Open the platform: EngiCalc →

What the platform does

  • Six disciplines: Fluid flow, heat transfer, strength, machine elements, pumps and acoustics, from pressure loss in pipes to Euler buckling.
  • Traceable results: Every result comes with its calculation path, so it can be checked and understood.
  • Runs in the browser: The calculators and the EngiBot assistant work locally, no input leaves the device.
  • Open source: The calculation core engicalc-core is available under the MIT license on GitHub and npm, with automated tests.
